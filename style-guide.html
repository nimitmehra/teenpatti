Style guide, proposal for approval

Plain, high contrast, and quietly royal.

Black ink on white paper, one walnut brown for authority, green for good news and brick red for caution. Royalty comes from the type and the spacing, not from gold or glitter.

Bullets and steps

A small diamond, like the card suit
Bullets
  • A run beats a colour. The opposite of poker.
  • K A 2 is not a run. The ace sits at the top or bottom only.
  • Suits do not rank. Spades are not higher than hearts.
Steps
  1. Everyone pays the boot.
  2. The dealer gives 3 cards each, face down.
  3. The player on the dealer’s left goes first.

How things move

Fast, small, never bouncy
Tap
The button sinks 1 pixel for an instant. You feel the press without anything flying around.
Next player
The old name fades up and out; the new name rises 8 pixels into place. About a fifth of a second.
Pot
The number counts up to its new total in under half a second, so everyone sees the chips go in.
Winner
The winner’s seat turns forest green. No confetti.
Calm setting
If the device is set to reduce motion, everything simply switches with no animation.
