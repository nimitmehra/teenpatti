# Teen Patti — The Family Card Table

A score keeper for learning and playing Teen Patti at home. Open it at **https://nimitmehra.com/teenpatti/**.

- Tells each player whose turn it is, which moves are allowed and what each costs (blind vs seen, chaal, raise, side show, show, pack).
- Keeps the pot, every move, and the running tally: who is up, who is down, and who pays whom.
- Rules sheet, hand rankings, and a card checker that decides who won.
- Runs entirely in the browser; games are saved on the device. No accounts, no server.

Files: `index.html` is the app. `DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md` and `style-guide-v2.html` describe the look. `DESIGN.md` covers the rules research and design decisions.
